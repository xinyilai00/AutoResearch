RELEVANT METHODOLOGIES:
- **Transformer variant benchmarking against traditional baselines** [10]: Oliveira & Ramos (2024) systematically evaluated vanilla Transformer, Informer, Autoformer, PatchTST, and Temporal Fusion Transformer (TFT) against AutoARIMA and AutoETS baselines for demand forecasting using MASE and weighted quantile loss (WQL) on the M5 competition dataset. This provides a methodological template for rigorous multi-model comparison with standardized error metrics, though applied to retail demand rather than financial data.
- **Simple linear/MLP models outperforming Transformers** [1][13]: Das et al. (2023) proposed TiDE, an MLP-based encoder-decoder that outperformed several Transformer-based approaches in long-term forecasting, demonstrating that architectural complexity does not guarantee superior performance. Li et al. (2026) further revealed that a single affine mapping (linear layer) can achieve competitive LTSF performance, suggesting that the purported advantages of Transformer attention mechanisms may be overstated for certain forecasting tasks. These findings directly challenge the premise that Transformer architectures should outperform simpler recurrent baselines.
