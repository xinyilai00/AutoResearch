CANDIDATE RESEARCH QUESTIONS:
1. How does the thematic composition of Cristiano Ronaldo's Instagram content—spanning training routines, luxury lifestyle, family life, and commercial endorsements—correlate with engagement patterns from self-identified teenage athletes, and which content categories most frequently elicit aspirational versus comparative commentary? | Gap addressed: Gap 1 — no study directly investigates how a single elite footballer's social media presence influences teenage athletes' training behaviors or athletic identity formation. | Feasibility: Ronaldo's Instagram account is public and among the most-followed globally; post metadata, captions, images, and public comments can be scraped and analyzed using NLP sentiment classification and topic modeling, with age-proxy filtering applied to commenter profiles.

2. To what extent do teenage participants in public youth football forums (e.g., Reddit r/bootroom, r/soccer, YouTube comment sections on Ronaldo-related training videos) articulate training behavior changes, skill emulation, or career aspiration shifts attributable to Ronaldo's documented career milestones such as club transfers, goal records, or public statements about discipline? | Gap addressed: Gap 2 — the intersection of athlete role modeling and adolescent athletic development remains unstudied, with no research connecting young fans' hero identification to actionable athletic development behaviors. | Feasibility: Public forum posts and video comments are freely accessible; NLP methods including named entity recognition, causal language detection, and thematic coding can identify self-reported behavioral changes attributed to Ronaldo across thousands of posts without requiring human subjects.

3. How do publicly observable discourses about Ronaldo's physique and training regimen among young social media users differ across at least three distinct cultural or national contexts (e.g., Brazil, the United Kingdom, and Southeast Asia), and do these discourses reflect culturally specific interpretations of athletic success? | Gap addressed: Gap 6 — cross-cultural and demographic variation in how teenage athletes respond to celebrity athlete influence is unexplored, with existing literature geographically fragmented and no comparative study conducted. | Feasibility: Geo-tagged or language-segmented social media data from public platforms can be collected and compared using cross-lingual NLP pipelines; publicly available cultural dimension indices (e.g., Hofstede) can serve as moderating variables in computational models.

4. What body image ideals and fitness norms are communicated through Ronaldo's curated visual content on Instagram, and how do young followers' comments reflect internalization, comparison, or resistance to these ideals relative to the body image pressures documented in general adolescent social media research (Dane & Bhatia, 2023; Feijóo et al., 2022)? | Gap addressed: Gap 3 — the social media and body image literature has not been applied to teenage athletes who follow elite sports figures, leaving unknown whether body image pressures are amplified or qualitatively different in this population. | Feasibility: Computer vision techniques can classify and quantify body-related visual content in Ronaldo's posts; NLP sentiment and framing analysis on public comments can detect comparison language, body dissatisfaction markers, and fitness aspiration expressions without requiring surveys or clinical data.

5. Do publicly expressed career expectations among young football enthusiasts who actively engage with Ronaldo-related content on social media align with or diverge from empirically documented probabilities of reaching professional football, as reported in publicly available career progression statistics and attrition data (Roderick, 2006; Williams & MacNamara, 2020)? | Gap addressed: Gap 5 — the potential for celebrity athlete influence to produce harmful unrealistic expectations among teenage athletes has not been investigated, and no research examines whether idolizing figures like Ronaldo leads to calibrated or distorted career probability beliefs. | Feasibility: Public youth football registration and progression statistics are available from national football associations; social media posts expressing career aspirations can be coded for expectation realism and compared against base-rate data using computational calibration analysis.

6. How has the volume, sentiment, and thematic focus of youth-oriented online discourse about Ronaldo evolved across key career transitions (e.g., Real Madrid to Juventus, Juventus to Manchester United, Manchester United to Al Nassr), and do these transitions correspond to measurable shifts in youth football discussion topics such as training motivation, career pathway beliefs, or commercial engagement? | Gap addressed: Gap 4 — no study employs longitudinal designs to assess how sustained exposure to a celebrity athlete's social media presence influences teenage athletes over time; existing work is predominantly cross-sectional. | Feasibility: Time-stamped public social media data and Google Trends indices enable longitudinal time-series analysis; structural break detection and sentiment trajectory modeling can identify discourse shifts aligned with Ronaldo's career events using entirely public data.

7. How do coaches, parents, and youth sport commentators in public online communities (e.g., youth football coaching forums, parenting subreddits, sports education blogs) frame Ronaldo's influence on young athletes, and do these mediating voices predominantly reinforce, contextualize, or counter the aspirational narratives promoted by Ronaldo's own media presence? | Gap addressed: Gap 7 — the role of mediating factors such as parental guidance, coaching environments, and peer influence in shaping how teenage athletes interpret celebrity athlete content has not been studied. | Feasibility: Public coaching forums, parenting communities, and sports education platforms contain extensive discussion threads that can be scraped and analyzed using discourse analysis and stance detection methods to classify adult framing of Ronaldo's influence without requiring direct access to private family or coaching interactions.

8. Compared to other globally prominent footballers (e.g., Messi, Mbappé, Haaland), does Ronaldo's social media presence generate a disproportionately higher volume of training emulation language, career aspiration references, and body comparison commentary among young users, and what content features account for any observed differential influence? | Gap addressed: Gap 1 — the athlete branding literature examines fan engagement and consumer outcomes but does not extend to developmental impacts on adolescent athletes who may aspire to emulate specific players, and no comparative analysis exists. | Feasibility: Public social media data for multiple athletes can be collected and compared using controlled NLP pipelines; engagement metrics, comment sentiment distributions, and topic prevalence can be statistically compared across athletes' follower bases using publicly observable interaction data.
