CANDIDATE RESEARCH QUESTIONS:
1. How do the lyrical themes of Drake and Kendrick Lamar differ in their treatment of race, masculinity, and emotional vulnerability, and to what extent do these thematic differences correspond to distinct patterns of engagement and sentiment among self-identified white teenage users on public social media platforms such as Reddit and Twitter? | Gap addressed: Gap 1 and Gap 3 — no study compares the differential influence of these two artists' distinct lyrical personas on any demographic, particularly white adolescents. | Feasibility: Full lyric corpora are publicly available via Genius and other lyric databases; NLP methods (topic modeling, sentiment analysis, LIWC) can quantify thematic content; Reddit and Twitter provide publicly accessible posts where users self-identify demographically and discuss these artists, enabling computational audience analysis without human subjects.

2. To what extent do Drake and Kendrick Lamar employ divergent linguistic styles and African-American English features in their discographies, and how does the frequency and context of these features correlate with cross-cultural linguistic adoption patterns observable in white teenage social media discourse? | Gap addressed: Gap 2 and Gap 3 — white teenagers as a distinct hip-hop audience are absent from the literature, and no research examines how contrasting artist styles affect cross-cultural transmission to this demographic. | Feasibility: Computational linguistics tools can detect AAE features in artist lyrics and in social media posts; public Twitter and Reddit corpora allow tracking of lexical diffusion patterns; (Chesley, 2011) provides a methodological precedent for studying AAE acquisition through hip-hop that can be adapted to computational analysis of public text data.

3. How do streaming platform metrics, search trends, and social media engagement data for Drake versus Kendrick Lamar differ across geographic regions with varying demographic compositions, and what do these patterns reveal about the relative reach of each artist among predominantly white adolescent populations? | Gap addressed: Gap 5 and Gap 7 — the role of platform curation and geographic/demographic moderators in shaping white teenagers' exposure to different hip-hop artists remains unexamined. | Feasibility: Google Trends data is publicly available and can be filtered by region; Spotify Charts and Billboard data provide streaming and sales metrics by market; U.S. Census data provides demographic composition by region; spatial analysis and regression can correlate artist popularity with demographic variables without requiring private platform data.

4. How do parasocial interaction indicators — such as first-person references, emotional disclosure, and identity claims — in white teenagers' public social media posts about Drake compare to those about Kendrick Lamar, and what do these differences suggest about the mediating role of parasocial relationships in message internalization? | Gap addressed: Gap 6 — the mediating role of parasocial relationships in how white teenagers internalize messages from these specific artists is entirely unaddressed in the literature. | Feasibility: Parasocial interaction can be operationalized through established linguistic markers detectable via NLP in public social media posts; Reddit communities (e.g., r/hiphopheads, r/Drake, r/KendrickLamar) and Twitter provide large corpora of fan discourse; computational parasocial interaction scales adapted from existing frameworks can be applied to text data without requiring surveys or human subjects.

5. How has the temporal evolution of Kendrick Lamar's socially conscious lyrical content — from Section.80 through Mr. Morale & The Big Steppers — influenced the thematic composition of discussions about race and social justice among white adolescent users on public forums, particularly following high-profile events such as the 2020 racial justice protests? | Gap addressed: Gap 4 — the literature lacks longitudinal analysis of how exposure to specific hip-hop artists' evolving messages affects white teenagers' attitudes over time. | Feasibility: Artist discographies with release dates provide a longitudinal corpus; Reddit and Twitter archives allow temporal analysis of user discourse; difference-in-differences or interrupted time-series designs can leverage exogenous events (album releases, protests) to measure shifts in discussion themes among demographically identifiable user cohorts using public data only.

6. In what ways do algorithmic recommendation structures on YouTube and Spotify create divergent exposure pathways for listeners who initially engage with Drake versus Kendrick Lamar, and how might these pathways differentially shape the breadth and nature of hip-hop content encountered by adolescent listeners? | Gap addressed: Gap 5 — the role of streaming platforms and algorithmic curation in mediating white teenagers' exposure to different hip-hop artists remains unexamined. | Feasibility: YouTube's recommendation graph can be crawled and analyzed through its public API; Spotify's "Fans Also Like" and related artist features are publicly visible; network analysis can map and compare the recommendation ecosystems surrounding each artist; simulation of listener journeys through these networks can model differential exposure without requiring proprietary algorithm access or human subjects.

7. How do the gender representations and portrayals of women in Drake's and Kendrick Lamar's lyrics and music videos differ quantitatively, and how do these differences correspond to the gender-related attitudes expressed by their respective white teenage fan communities in online discourse? | Gap addressed: Gap 3 and Gap 1 — no research examines how contrasting cultural positions of these artists, particularly regarding gender representation, differentially affect the attitudes of white adolescent listeners. | Feasibility: Lyrics are publicly available; music videos are publicly accessible on YouTube and can be coded using established content analysis frameworks for gender representation (building on Lewis, 2010 and Robillard, 2012); NLP sentiment and theme analysis of fan discussions on Reddit and Twitter can quantify gender-related attitudes expressed in each artist's fan community.

8. To what extent do white teenagers' online discussions of Drake versus Kendrick Lamar reflect distinct frameworks for understanding racial identity and cultural appropriation, and how do these frameworks vary across online communities with different ideological orientations? | Gap addressed: Gap 7 — there is no research examining how white teenagers' preexisting attitudes and community contexts moderate the influence of hip-hop artists on their worldview regarding race. | Feasibility: Public Reddit communities span a range of ideological orientations and can be analyzed computationally; framing analysis and discourse analysis can be automated using NLP; demographic inference methods allow identification of likely white adolescent users; comparative analysis across communities can reveal how preexisting community norms moderate engagement with artist messages, all using publicly available data.
